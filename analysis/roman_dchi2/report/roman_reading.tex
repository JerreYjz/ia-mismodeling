No variant moves the Roman cut. Every model crosses FoB = 0.3 between
$K_0 = 0.35$ and 0.4 in the $1/D(z)$ family (0.41 to 0.53 at $K_0 = 0.4$) and
sits at 0.26 to 0.32 at the constant $k_{\rm UV} = 0.4$. F21 and F21 red/blue
agree to 0.02: the decoupling parameter fits to $r = 0.97$ to 1.12, so Roman
does not use it. Adding the satellites to TATT (last column against the TATT
column) changes the FoB by at most 0.05: the satellite amplitude is
$\lesssim 0.01$ at most masks, and where it is larger (0.03 to 0.9, with
$\eta_{1h}$ at its bound) it is a flat direction. Without density weighting
the blue tidal-torquing hybrid behaves like NLA (0.29 / 0.32 at $K_0 = 0.35$).
Caveat: in this pipeline (IA held at the fiducial cosmology, cosmology through
the lensing response) TATT comes out 0.03 to 0.08 above NLA at $K_0 = 0.3$ to
0.35, while the full native fits of the strategies note had them close (case B:
NLA 0.25 and TATT 0.29 at $K_0 = 0.3$; 0.32 and 0.31 at 0.35). The frozen-IA
approximation is worse for TATT than for NLA ($\pm 0.03$), so compare the
columns with each other, not with the strategies note.
